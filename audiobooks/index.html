WHAT IT DOES

Press play, wherever you left off.

Listen

A player made for books, not songs.

  • Built-in player with a chapter list — .m4b chapters included
  • Speed control, and skip forward and back
  • A sleep timer
  • Bookmarks at the moments you want to come back to
  • Mark a book finished and it leaves Continue listening

Pick up anywhere

Your place is yours, and it follows you.

  • Your position saved as you listen, per book and per person
  • Stop on the phone, carry on at the laptop
  • Continue listening on Home
  • Download a book and listen with no connection
  • Installs on your phone like an app

Find the next one

Browse the way you think about books.

  • Authors, narrators, series and categories
  • Search by title, author and series
  • Filters by narrator, length, tag and finished
  • An A–Z index, with an EN / RU switch for mixed libraries
  • Covers compact, comfortable or large

Your folders, understood

No re-tagging and no renaming before you start.

  • One folder, one book — CD 1 and Part 2 folders fold into one
  • Titles, authors, narrators and covers from the files’ own tags
  • Layouts for Author / Series / Title folders, with a preview first
  • Look a title up in Audible, Open Library, Apple Books, LibriVox or FantLab
  • Your edits win over every later scan

PRIVATE HERE, SPECIFICALLY

What you listen to is nobody else’s business.

The general promises hold everywhere. These are the ones that matter most for audiobooks.

Your listening stays at home

Where you are in each book, your bookmarks and what you’ve finished are kept on your server, per person. There are no listening statistics going anywhere.

Lookups only when you ask

Looking a title up online happens in the editor, when you press search, and asks for the words you typed. Nothing scans your library against an online service.

Offline really is offline

A downloaded book plays with no connection at all — nothing has to check in before it starts.

Your files are only read

Corrections live in iSputnik and survive every re-scan. The audio files themselves are read, never rewritten.

QUESTIONS

About audiobooks.

More in the Audiobooks guide.

Do I have to rename or re-tag my files?

No. Each folder becomes one book, named from its tags or, failing that, from the folder. Deeper arrangements like Author / Series / Title are taught once, in the library’s Layout, with a preview before anything changes.

Does it understand .m4b chapters?

Yes. The chapter marks inside an .m4b are read and listed. For a book made of many MP3s, each file becomes a chapter.

Can two of us listen to the same book?

Yes. Everyone has their own account, so each person’s place, bookmarks and finished books are their own.

Can I listen without a connection?

Yes. Download the book from its page in the installed app, and it plays anywhere — a commute, a flight, a cabin.
