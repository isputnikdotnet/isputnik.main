WHAT IT DOES

From the shoebox to the big screen.

Find any moment

Your folders, read where they are, turned into a gallery you can actually browse.

  • A timeline of every photo and video, by date
  • Albums, folders and favourites
  • Memories: this day in years gone by
  • A map of where they were taken, and a Places view
  • Search and filters by person, place, year and tag
  • Videos play in the browser, even formats it can’t open by itself

Bring them all home

Every phone, every relative, every old print — into one place, without copies piling up.

  • Phone backup into each person’s own private library
  • Uploads filed into dated folders for you
  • Photo Inbox for scans and relatives’ photos, with a copy check
  • Drop links so family without an account can send you photos
  • Move a folder to another library without breaking a thing

Remember together

The people who were there know the stories. Make it easy for them to tell them.

  • Review mode: one photo at a time — when, where, who, what you remember
  • Dictate instead of typing, or keep a voice recording on a photo
  • Notes under a photo for the next person who opens it
  • Send a photo or album to family, or by an expiring guest link
  • Slideshows with music and a title card, rendered to MP4

Keep it tidy

Big collections get messy. Tidy them up without ever being afraid to.

  • Face recognition that groups the family into people
  • Duplicate cleanup that remembers every decision you made
  • Near-identical photos flagged as a judgement call, never removed for you
  • A recycle bin for anything removed, until you empty it
  • Folder locks that keep precious folders out of every cleanup

PRIVATE HERE, SPECIFICALLY

Your photos don’t leave the house.

The general promises hold everywhere. These are the ones that matter most for photos.

Faces are found at home

Face recognition is off until you turn it on, and then it runs on your own server, in the faces container beside iSputnik. Not one photo is sent away to be analysed.

Your phone, your library

Phone backup goes straight from the phone to your server, over the home Wi-Fi or your VPN — never mobile data, never someone’s cloud. It lands in a library only you can open; administrators see how full it is, never what’s in it.

Maps without being watched

Turn on offline maps and the map comes through your server and is kept there, so the map provider no longer sees where in the world you’re looking.

Place names worked out at home

“Verona, Veneto, Italy” comes from a place-name database your server downloads once. After that, your photos’ locations are never sent anywhere to be looked up.

Originals are never touched

iSputnik reads your folders where they are. Deleting a photo on the phone leaves the copy at home, and anything a cleanup removes waits in the recycle bin.

QUESTIONS

About photos.

More in the Gallery guide and Backing up your phone.

Do I have to move or reorganise my photos?

No. A gallery library shows whatever is in its folder, the way you already keep it. Photos copied there appear after the next scan, and the originals are never renamed, moved or deleted.

Who can see the photos my phone backs up?

Only you. They go into your own library, which not even the administrators can open. Photos you move into a family library are seen by whoever can open that one.

If I delete a photo on my phone, is it deleted at home too?

No. Phone backup only ever adds, so free up the phone as you like. A lost phone is revoked in one click, and what it sent stays.

Can my phone back up when I’m away from home?

Only through a VPN into the house. Never over mobile data or the open internet — the phone simply catches up when you’re home.
