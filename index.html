Self-hosted family media library

isputnik.home

An open source home server that keeps your family's audiobooks, ebooks, photos, and videos in one place — organized, searchable, and shared only with the people you choose.

  • Runs on your own machine — Docker image and Unraid template included
  • Family accounts with per-user progress, permissions, and profiles
  • Installable as a mobile app (PWA) with offline listening
  • Your media, metadata, and backups never leave your control

In depth

A closer look

🎬

Slideshows that become movies

Pick photos and videos, choose a transition — crossfade, slide, Ken Burns, dip to black — set the pace, and lay your own music underneath. The server even suggests slideshows on its own, clustering moments from dates, places, and the people in them, with burst shots collapsed to one photo.

  • Render to a real MP4 movie (1080p) as a background job, with live progress
  • Title card options: plain black, a photo, a blurred photo, or a collage
  • Finished movies file themselves into a gallery library — re-renders replace, never pile up
🧹

Duplicate photo cleanup

Phone backups run twice, cards get imported into two places — libraries collect copies. A four-step wizard scans the libraries you pick, groups the copies, and lets you work through them a job at a time, at your own pace.

  • Resumable: close the browser, come back next week, your decisions are kept
  • Folder instructions — mark a folder's copies as the keepers, or as safe to clear
  • Everything removed lands in the Recycle Bin until you empty it deliberately
🛡️

Security, taken seriously

Built for a home network, hardened for the internet. Two-factor sign-in works with an authenticator app or emailed codes, with backup codes for when the phone is gone — and the server watches its own front door.

  • Account lockout, IP blocking, and trusted zones for your home network
  • Rate limiting, a strict Content Security Policy, and HTTPS support with HSTS
  • Email alerts to admins, a full activity log, and a step-by-step exposure guide
📴

Offline, on your phone

Install it from the browser like an app — phone-style bottom navigation included. Download audiobooks to the device and keep listening on the plane, in the woods, or wherever the server can't follow.

  • Downloaded books keep their details and player working fully offline
  • Progress made offline syncs back when you reconnect
  • Private caches are wiped on logout, so a shared phone shares nothing
