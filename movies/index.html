WHAT IT DOES

Everything you’d expect. Nothing watching you.

Browse

Your folders, dressed like a streaming service.

  • Films and shows from the folders Jellyfin and Kodi read
  • Posters, backdrops and descriptions from .nfo files or TMDB
  • Cast & crew pages, genres and film sets
  • Rows you arrange yourself, beside Continue and Recently added
  • Search, filters and an A–Z index
  • Edit any title’s details by hand

Watch

Press play. The server works out the rest.

  • Plays in any browser — what it can’t play is converted as you watch
  • Sound tracks and subtitles, inside the film or beside it
  • Skip recap, skip intro, and the next episode at the credits
  • Continue watching and watched marks, per person
  • Mark a whole show or season watched

Away from home

Your films go where you go.

  • Quality capped for viewers outside the house, 720p to start
  • Pick 1080p, 720p or 480p for a weak Wi-Fi corner
  • Download a film or a whole season to watch offline
  • Smaller copies prepared for you, with an email when they’re ready

The household

One server, everyone’s own way of watching.

  • Profiles like Kids, Grandma or Russian, each with its own history
  • A PIN that keeps Kids in Kids
  • Who may watch, and from where: home only, trusted networks or anywhere
  • Which libraries each person sees
  • Converts on an Intel GPU, with HDR made right for ordinary screens

PRIVATE HERE, SPECIFICALLY

Watch what you like. Nobody’s counting.

The general promises hold everywhere. These are the ones that matter most for movies & TV.

Nothing reported about what you watch

Progress, watched marks and history are kept on your server, per person and per profile. There’s no one on the other end to report to.

Film details with your own key

Posters and descriptions come from your .nfo files first, then TMDB with a free key of your own. TMDB is asked about titles — never sent a file.

No door of its own

The video engine has no pages and no port open to the internet. Everyone goes through iSputnik’s sign-in, two-factor included.

Your files are only read

The video folders are mounted read-only. Deleting a library forgets its catalogue and leaves every file where it was.

QUESTIONS

About movies & TV.

More in Adding the games, video and faces apps.

Do I have to rename my files?

Probably not. It reads the layout Jellyfin and Kodi read, takes .nfo files first, and fills in the rest from the names — release names included.

Will it play my .mkv files?

Yes. What a browser can play goes as it is; the rest is converted while you watch — only the sound when the picture is fine.

Can we watch away from home?

Yes, at up to 720p to start with: one viewer needs about 5 Mbit/s of your upload. Or download a film before you go.

Does it need a graphics card?

No. An Intel processor’s graphics help a lot when several people watch converted films at once.
